## Context

This is the part of the platform buyers actually see. It runs as a standalone shop or embedded in a producer's own
website, and it has to handle all the messy details of selling fresh food.

## What I built

- **Catalogue.** Product variations with the correct units, stock levels, labels and a category for services.
- **Delivery & pickup.** Slot selection that adapts to each producer's schedule.
- **Pricing that adds up.** Promotions, coupons and tiered percentage discounts. VAT is handled per rate, including
  delivery rates, and totals are rounded so they always match the invoice.
- **Payments.** I redesigned the payment experience, added a payment summary before checkout, and made payment links
  and embedded payments work smoothly.
- **Embedded mode.** Works inside a producer's own website, with mobile behaviour tuned for embedding.
- **Multilingual.** Fully translated, with correct pluralisation.
