## The problem

Small food producers need a web presence, but most don't have the time or budget to build and maintain a website.

## What I built

A multi-tenant **Next.js** app that generates a website for each producer from data they already keep on the
platform.

- **Custom domains.** Producers can connect their own domain, with automatic redirects to the canonical address.
- **Themeable.** Colours and titles follow each producer's branding, and a preview mode lets them see changes before
  they go live.
- **Content blocks.** Pages are built from structured blocks, including conditional ones that only appear when the
  producer has relevant data.
- **Lead capture.** A built-in contact form turns visitors into customers, styled to match the site.
- **Compliance.** Legal notices are generated from the producer's own details, and analytics only load after cookie
  consent.
- **Multilingual.** Translations are handled with per-locale dictionaries.
