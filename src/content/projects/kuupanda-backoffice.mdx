## Purchase invoices

Producers need to keep track of what they owe, not just what they sell. I built the purchase invoices module in the
admin app:

- Supplier invoices can be entered manually or **received as electronic invoices**.
- Accounting codes, with VAT split per rate so the figures match the invoice totals.
- Matching against bank transactions to show what has been paid.
- Due dates, accounting export and a mobile-friendly layout.

On the sales side, I added credit invoices, deposit invoices, batch invoice generation and bulk sending of invoices
and delivery notes.

## Customer messaging

Producers talk to their customers regularly, for example about weekly availability, pickup reminders and order updates.
I built the messaging module:

- Compose and send messages to individual customers or groups.
- **Scheduled and recurring** messages that go out automatically.
- Clear statuses for messages that are scheduled, sent and cancelled.
