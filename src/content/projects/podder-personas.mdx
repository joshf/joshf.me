## Audience personas

Background jobs combine LLM classification with audience data to build a listener persona for each podcast. That
gives podcasters and their partners a clear picture of who is listening.

## Review insights

The same jobs summarise listener reviews and their sentiment, and identify and enrich guest information for each episode.

## AI assistant

The dashboard includes an AI chat assistant, so users can ask questions about their shows in plain English.
